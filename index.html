01

Everything it does

The core features at a glance.

01

A closed shadow-DOM overlay is drawn over each page, so unlocking removes one layer instead of reloading the tab.

02

The toolbar button covers every window at once, and VeilMate registers no keyboard shortcut at all, so it never competes for your keys.

03

Covered pages swallow clicks, scrolling, typing and wheel events, so nothing behind the overlay reacts.

04

New tabs, new windows including private ones, and addresses typed while locked are all caught and sent to the lock screen.

05

The lock lives in local storage, so it is still there after a browser restart until you unlock it.

06

Passwords are derived with PBKDF2-SHA256 at 310,000 iterations and never leave the device.

Also included

  • Idle auto-lock covers the browser after ten minutes without input, and five wrong passwords put the lock screen in a thirty-second cooldown.
  • The lock screen shows a large clock, so you can read the time without revealing what you were working on.
  • Six interface languages: English, 简体中文, 日本語, Deutsch, Español, Français.

02

Free vs Pro

The free tier is a complete lock, not a trial. Pro is optional.

Free

One-click lock, the lossless overlay, password unlock, a lock that survives a browser restart, idle auto-lock, a ten-domain exempt list, the new-tab and address-bar trap, the large clock, brute-force cooldown and six languages.

Pro

Targeted lock that covers only the domains you list, biometric unlock with Windows Hello or Touch ID, focus-loss lock, system-lock link, lock at browser start, the privacy curtain, the lock activity log, wallpaper and a custom lock-screen message, and cross-device sync including the domain list.

No account and no licence are needed to lock your browser. Pro is optional.

Private by design

Your tabs stay on your device. No screenshots, no page content, no browsing history leaves the browser. Read exactly what is stored and when the extension touches the network.
